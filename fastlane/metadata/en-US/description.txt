Cull, edit and deliver a photo shoot without a catalogue. Photopipe reads the folders you already have and writes every decision back as standard XMP.

CULL FAST
• Full-bleed loupe, rate with 1 to 5, move with the arrow keys
• Instinct score: every frame rated 0 to 100 on your Mac by Apple's aesthetics model, so the first pass is already done
• Rating histogram that doubles as the filter

A REAL RAW EDIT
• Exposure, highlights, shadows, whites and blacks
• White balance in true Kelvin on raw files, tint, vibrance and saturation
• Texture, clarity and dehaze
• Tone curves for RGB and each channel
• Crop, straighten and rotate, non-destructive
• Denoise on the Neural Engine
• Copy a look from one photo and paste it onto the whole selection

DELIVER
• Originals byte for byte, or full-resolution JPEG with every edit baked in
• Folder or zip, flat or mirroring your subfolders, never a silent overwrite
• Export jobs stay visible, with errors you can still read minutes later

YOUR FILES STAY YOURS
• No catalogue, no import, no account, no telemetry
• Ratings and edits travel as XMP sidecars that other photo apps read
• Delete means the Trash, never an unlink

Free and open source. Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple Silicon.
