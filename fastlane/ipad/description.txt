Cull, edit and deliver a photo shoot on your iPad without a catalogue. Photopipe works on the folders you already have in Files and writes every decision back as standard XMP.

BRING IT IN
• From a card reader or drive in Files, or from your Photos library
• Raws stay raws; a project is just a folder

CULL FAST
• Full-bleed loupe with a filmstrip, rate 1 to 5 with a tap or the keyboard
• Instinct score: every frame rated 0 to 100 on your iPad by Apple's aesthetics model
• Rating histogram that doubles as the filter

A REAL RAW EDIT
• Exposure, highlights, shadows, whites and blacks
• White balance in true Kelvin, tint, vibrance and saturation
• Texture, clarity and dehaze
• Tone curves for RGB and each channel
• Crop, straighten and rotate, non-destructive
• Copy a look from one photo and paste it onto the whole selection

DELIVER
• Originals byte for byte, or full-resolution JPEG with every edit baked in
• Into any folder, flat or mirroring your subfolders, never a silent overwrite

YOUR FILES STAY YOURS
• No catalogue, no account, no telemetry
• Ratings and edits travel as XMP sidecars that other photo apps read

Free and open source. Requires iPadOS 18 or later.
